To evaluate continuous early warning without reconstructing timing from labels, I would need de-identified continuous recordings and synchronized FoG onset/offset annotations, if these can be shared under your study's permissions.

Could you clarify whether the following are available?

- Raw or causally processed ankle accelerometer/gyroscope recordings with sample timestamps and explicit missing-data intervals.
- FoG onset/offset times and the annotation/synchronization uncertainty.
- Stable participant and session identifiers linking recordings, including the relationship between file names such as PD001 and internal metadata identifiers such as PD019.
- Sensor units, axis conventions, filtering direction and acquisition sampling rate.
- Sufficient continuous non-FoG activity for estimating false warnings per hour.

I would follow the dataset's access conditions and cite the study. If these recordings cannot be shared, any clarification about timing and preprocessing in the public release would still be valuable.
