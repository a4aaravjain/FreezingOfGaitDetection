# Why published headline results do not establish this project's target

The previous system is not demonstrated to be competitive with the best comparable published predictor. Adapting a network topology with fresh weights, different sensors, a different target and a much smaller study is not reproduction of the complete published system. Better published methods justify further work, but do not establish that this particular ankle signal supports near-perfect two-second prediction with fewer than one unsuccessful warning/hour.

- Borzi et al.2025 [official paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1877718X241302766): the reported88–95% external episode detection includes warnings at/after onset. In its internal test,25% were predicted an average2s early,25% at onset,37.5% about1s late;65.3% of recognized episodes were false positives. Thus the headline is not88–95% sensitivity at>=2s and<1 unsuccessful/hour. The model used a left-ankle gyroscope; current deployed input is a right-ankle six-axis IMU.
- Pardoel et al.2022 [official paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2022.831063/full): bilateral plantar-pressure models identified94.2% of episodes, with mean participant-average lead0.8s and2.4 false positives/walking trial. This is useful early-prediction evidence, but changes sensor modality, warning horizon and alarm metric. Window sensitivity/specificity and episode identification also differ.
- Wang et al.2025 [indexed primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40030782/),doi10.1109/JBHI.2024.3522664: explicitly studies early prediction and lightweight distillation. An accessible official full protocol/code was not established in this audit, so no numerical equivalence or reproduction claim is made. It is incorrect to dismiss all published work as merely detection.

Our OR-only design imposed a mathematical false-warning floor and was an unsuitable architecture for the requested joint target. The challenger removes that constraint. The earlier candidate count was also misinterpreted:67/72 had candidates,65/72 received direct pulses, and a risk-blind quarter-capacity schedule covered71/72. High candidate coverage is not evidence by itself that a selective precursor has been learned.

The new comparison keeps every original onset, known exposure and physical-session boundary fixed. Historical development reuse still limits independence. No current worldwide "best" rank can be inferred by comparing incompatible datasets and metrics.
