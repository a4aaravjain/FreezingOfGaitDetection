# Event-hazard research and source admission

All downloaded files remain read-only under external_sources/event_hazard_20261111_01. No downloaded code, preprocessing, weights or datasets are executed/imported. Implementations are original compact Keras adaptations. Exact commits, individual file hashes and inspected reference files are recorded in external_repository_audit.json. Prior source audit and upstream models remain frozen and are reused, never retrained.

Hazard likelihood references: [Gensheimer and Narasimhan2019](https://peerj.com/articles/6257/), [Kvamme and Borgan2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10985-021-09532-6). These are survival methods, not FoG performance claims. Unknown future tails are never no-event labels; an observed onset may still have a known event bin in a partially followed tail.

[DANN, JMLR2016](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v17/15-239.html) and its [official code](https://github.com/GRAAL-Research/domain_adversarial_neural_network) were screened as background on subject shift. Its target-domain adaptation is not admitted: no outer participant training statistics or transductive model fitting are allowed. This study uses fitting-subject-balanced sampling and a separately declared label-blind first20s setup; it does not claim proven subject invariance or an implemented DANN.

Alert constraints are calibrated on complete replayed starts, not window specificity. The controller's short3s refractory and soft60s-decaying burden adjustment do not impose an hour-long cooldown or guarantee a rate by silencing the wearer. Final starts, unknown starts and all missed setup onsets are scored.

The auxiliary standing/non-FoG head is explicitly a stopping proxy because intention to stop is unannotated. Periodicity disruption is a trailing-signal proxy. Neither is claimed to be independently annotated clinical ground truth. Activity, current FoG and post-FoG labels enter fitting losses only.
